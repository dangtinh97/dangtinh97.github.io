DimoHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Summary: DimoHub uses location information to provide location-based road and traffic warning features. Location coordinates may be transmitted to our servers to provide these services. The app also uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to communicate with compatible HUB devices and may use Apple Maps and Google Maps to display map information.

1. Introduction

DimoHub ("DimoHub", "we", "us", or "our") is an application designed to provide road, traffic, speed, and warning information to users while traveling. The application can display warning signs and road information, show the user's location on a map, and communicate with compatible HUB hardware devices using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).

This Privacy Policy explains what information DimoHub may collect, how that information is used, how it is transmitted, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Location Information

DimoHub requests access to your device's location services. Depending on the permissions you grant, the application may receive approximate or precise location information, including:

  • Latitude and longitude;
  • Current movement or speed information provided by the device;
  • Heading or direction information, where available;
  • Other location-related information necessary to provide the application's features.

Location information is used to provide location-based functionality, including identifying the user's current position, obtaining nearby road and traffic warnings, determining relevant speed or road information, displaying the user's position on a map, and providing information that may be transmitted to a connected HUB device.

2.2 Transmission of Location Information

DimoHub may transmit your latitude and longitude, together with other relevant location or road information, to our servers. This transmission is necessary for location-based services such as retrieving traffic warnings, road information, warning signs, and other information relevant to your current location.

We use transmitted location information only for providing and operating the application's location-based features, improving service reliability, security, and performance, and maintaining the functionality of the service.

Important: DimoHub does not use location information for advertising or to sell your location information to third parties.

2.3 Bluetooth and BLE Information

DimoHub uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to discover, connect to, and communicate with compatible HUB hardware devices.

Depending on the connected device and enabled features, information transmitted over BLE may include road warnings, traffic information, speed information, speed limits, road names, warning sign information, device commands, and device status information.

BLE communication is used to operate and communicate with compatible hardware. DimoHub does not use Bluetooth communication to track your physical location independently of the location services described in this Privacy Policy.

2.4 Device and Technical Information

The application and its servers may process technical information needed to operate the service, such as application version, operating system version, device type, connection information, and diagnostic information when necessary for security, troubleshooting, and service operation.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by DimoHub may be used to:

  • Provide location-based road and traffic warnings;
  • Display your current position and relevant information on maps;
  • Provide speed and speed-limit related information;
  • Identify relevant road signs and warnings near your location;
  • Transmit relevant information to a compatible HUB through BLE;
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the application and its services;
  • Detect and prevent abuse, unauthorized access, or technical problems;
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Maps and Third-Party Services

4.1 Apple Maps

DimoHub may use Apple Maps and Apple's mapping technologies to display maps, roads, locations, and other geographic information. Apple may process information in accordance with Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

4.2 Google Maps

DimoHub may also use Google Maps or Google mapping technologies to display maps, roads, locations, and geographic information. Google may process information in accordance with Google's own privacy policies and terms.

The use of third-party mapping services does not mean that DimoHub sells your personal information to those providers. However, when such services are used, their respective systems may process technical or location information according to their own policies.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or location information for advertising purposes.

Information may be processed or transmitted to service providers that are necessary to operate DimoHub, including hosting, infrastructure, mapping, security, and other technical service providers.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, court orders, protect the security of the service, or prevent fraud, abuse, or other harmful activity.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant service, maintain security, troubleshoot technical issues, comply with legal obligations, or fulfill legitimate operational purposes.

Location information transmitted to our servers may be temporarily processed to provide location-based features. The actual retention period may depend on the technical operation of the service and the specific feature being used.

We do not retain information longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, except where a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by DimoHub against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Permissions and Your Choices

DimoHub requests only the permissions needed to provide its features. You can manage permissions through the privacy and settings controls provided by iOS.

Permission / Service Purpose
Location Services Determine your location, provide road and traffic warnings, calculate or display relevant speed information, and provide location-based map features.
Bluetooth Discover, connect to, and communicate with compatible DimoHub HUB devices.
Maps Display geographic information, roads, locations, and map-related content.

If you deny or disable Location Services, some or all location-based features may not function correctly. If you deny or disable Bluetooth, communication with compatible HUB devices will not be available.

9. Precise and Approximate Location

iOS allows you to provide an application with either precise or approximate location information. DimoHub may require precise location information for features that depend on accurate road position, speed-related information, or nearby warnings.

If you choose to provide only approximate location information, certain features may be less accurate or may not work as intended.

10. Background Location

DimoHub may continue to access the device's location while the application is running in the background when the user has granted the required iOS location permissions and the relevant system capabilities are enabled.

Background location access is used to provide continuous location-based features while the user is traveling, including monitoring the user's current position, obtaining relevant road and traffic warnings, updating speed or road information, and providing relevant information to a connected HUB device.

When background location is enabled, location information may continue to be transmitted to our servers for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. DimoHub does not use background location information for advertising or sell location information to third parties.

Users can control or disable location permissions through iOS Settings. Disabling background location access may prevent DimoHub from providing continuous location-based features while the application is not in the foreground.

11. Background Bluetooth Communication

DimoHub may use Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) communication with compatible HUB devices while the application is in the background, where supported by iOS, the connected device, and the current Bluetooth connection state.

Background Bluetooth communication is used to maintain or resume communication with a compatible HUB and to provide relevant warning, road, speed, or device information. iOS may limit or suspend background Bluetooth activity according to its system policies and resource management.

DimoHub does not use background Bluetooth communication to independently track the user's physical location.

12. Account Information

DimoHub does not require you to create an account solely to use the core location, map, warning, or HUB communication features.

If account-based features are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to describe the additional information collected and how it is used.

13. Children's Privacy

DimoHub is not intended to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a manner that violates applicable privacy laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

14. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your data.

You may also withdraw location or Bluetooth permissions through your device settings. Withdrawing a permission may prevent certain DimoHub features from working.

15. Data Deletion Requests

If DimoHub stores personal information associated with your use of the service and you wish to request its deletion, please contact us using the contact information below.

We may need to verify a request before processing it. Certain information may be retained when required by law, necessary for security, or needed to resolve disputes or enforce our agreements.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to DimoHub, applicable laws, or our data practices.

When changes are made, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
