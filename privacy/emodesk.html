Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 18, 2026

EmoDesk ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how EmoDesk collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Voice audio captured after the wake word ("Hey Emo") is activated.
  • Microphone access solely for voice interaction.
  • Basic technical information required to operate the service, such as device and application version.

2. Microphone Usage

EmoDesk listens locally for the "Hey Emo" wake word. After activation, the microphone captures your voice so you can communicate with EmoDesk. Voice audio is transmitted as binary data over a secure network connection for speech recognition and AI processing. The microphone is not used for any unrelated purpose.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide voice conversations with EmoDesk.
  • Convert speech into text.
  • Generate AI responses.
  • Improve application reliability and performance.

4. Data Security

We use secure communication channels to protect transmitted data. Reasonable technical measures are implemented to help safeguard your information against unauthorized access.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your data may be processed by trusted service providers only when necessary to provide the application's functionality.

6. Your Choices

  • You may deny microphone permission, although voice features will not function.
  • You may stop using the application at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

EmoDesk is not intended for children under the age required by applicable laws without parental supervision.
