Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Lensly need camera access?
Camera access allows you to take photos directly in Lensly for AI-powered analysis. Camera access is only used when you choose a feature that requires it.
Why does Lensly need photo library access?
Photo library access allows you to select existing photos that you want to analyze with Lensly.
Are my photos automatically shared with the community?
No. Photos are not automatically published to the community. You choose whether to share an analyzed photo.
How can I delete a photo from my history?
You can delete photos and their associated analysis from your History using the delete function provided in Lensly.
How can I delete a community post?
You can delete your own community posts using the delete function provided in Lensly.
How do I report a problem?
Please contact us by email and describe the problem, including the Lensly feature you were using and any relevant details that may help us investigate.
